The Famagusta region of southeastern Cyprus encompasses the popular resort areas of Ayia Napa and Protaras, where the island's coastline reveals some of its most striking natural features. This corner of Cyprus is defined by its dramatic meeting of land and sea, with rugged headlands, hidden sea caves, and unspoiled coastal scenery that attracts both nature enthusiasts and beach-goers. The area serves as a natural corridor for bird migrations, adding seasonal wildlife interest to the already compelling landscape. Visitors to this region can expect a blend of natural beauty and coastal exploration opportunities along the island's southeastern shores.
