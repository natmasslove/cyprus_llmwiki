# Regions

- [Paphos](/regions/paphos.md) - Ancient ruins, sacred sites, and mythological landmarks around Cyprus's historic coastal city
- [Limassol](/regions/limassol.md) - Ancient ruins, medieval castles, and sacred sites around Cyprus's southern coast
- [Larnaca](/regions/larnaca.md) - Ancient sacred sites and natural wonders around Cyprus's historic coastal city
- [Troodos](/regions/troodos.md) - Byzantine religious heritage and UNESCO-listed painted churches in Cyprus's mountain heartland
- [Famagusta (Ayia Napa & Protaras)](/regions/famagusta.md) - Coastal destinations in southeastern Cyprus centered around Ayia Napa and Protaras, featuring dramatic natural landscapes.
