The Troodos region stands as the spiritual and artistic heart of Byzantine Cyprus, where centuries of Orthodox Christian tradition have left an extraordinary legacy in the island's mountainous interior. This area is home to some of Cyprus's most significant religious monuments, including the grand Kykkos Monastery with its revered icon and a remarkable collection of ten Byzantine churches recognized by UNESCO for their exceptional medieval murals. Visitors to Troodos will encounter a landscape where faith, art, and history converge, offering insight into the island's deep religious roots and the artistic achievements of Byzantine civilization. The region's churches and monasteries preserve not only architectural treasures but also some of the finest examples of Byzantine religious painting found anywhere in the Mediterranean.

# Sites
- [Kykkos Monastery](/sites/kykkos-monastery.md) - One of Cyprus's wealthiest monasteries, founded in the 11th century, housing an icon attributed to the Apostle Luke.
- [Painted Churches in the Troodos Region](/sites/painted-churches-troodos.md) - UNESCO World Heritage Site comprising ten Byzantine churches with murals in the Troödos Mountains of central Cyprus.
