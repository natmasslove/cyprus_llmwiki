# Sites

- [Agios Neophytos Monastery](/sites/agios-neophytos-monastery.md) - A 12th-century monastery founded by hermit monk Neophytos, carved into rock near Paphos with Byzantine frescoes and cave hermitage.
- [Cape Greco](/sites/cape-greco.md) - A dramatic headland in southeastern Cyprus, known for its sea caves, bird migrations, and natural coastal beauty.
- [Choirokoitia](/sites/choirokoitia.md) - A UNESCO World Heritage Neolithic settlement from 7000 BC, one of the eastern Mediterranean's best-preserved prehistoric sites.
- [Church of Saint Lazarus, Larnaca](/sites/church-of-saint-lazarus.md) - A late-9th century Greek Orthodox church in Larnaca built over the reputed tomb of Lazarus of Bethany.
- [Hala Sultan Tekke](/sites/hala-sultan-tekke.md) - A mosque and mausoleum complex on Larnaca Salt Lake, considered one of Islam's most important pilgrimage sites.
- [Kolossi Castle](/sites/kolossi-castle.md) - A 15th-century Crusader castle near Limassol, once a strategic stronghold and center of sugar production and Commandaria wine.
- [Kourion](/sites/kourion.md) - Ancient Greek city-state on Cyprus's southwestern coast, with extensive Roman ruins atop a dramatic coastal promontory.
- [Kykkos Monastery](/sites/kykkos-monastery.md) - One of Cyprus's wealthiest monasteries, founded in the 11th century, housing an icon attributed to the Apostle Luke.
- [Larnaca Salt Lake](/sites/larnaca-salt-lake.md) - A complex of four interconnected salt lakes west of Larnaca, Cyprus's second-largest salt lake and an important wetland for migratory birds.
- [Limassol Castle](/sites/limassol-castle.md) - A medieval castle in Limassol's old harbour, rebuilt by the Ottomans in 1590 over earlier Christian and Byzantine structures.
- [Painted Churches in the Troodos Region](/sites/painted-churches-troodos.md) - UNESCO World Heritage Site comprising ten Byzantine churches with murals in the Troödos Mountains of central Cyprus.
- [Paphos Archaeological Park](/sites/paphos-archaeological-park.md) - Major archaeological site in Cyprus preserving the ancient capital of Nea Paphos with exceptional Roman mosaics and urban remains.
- [Petra tou Romiou](/sites/petra-tou-romiou.md) - A sea stack off the coast of Paphos, Cyprus, known in mythology as the birthplace of Aphrodite.
- [Sanctuary of Apollo Hylates](/sites/apollo-hylates.md) - Ancient sanctuary where Apollo Hylates, god of the woodlands, was worshipped from the eighth century BC to the fourth century AD.
- [Tombs of the Kings (Paphos)](/sites/tombs-of-the-kings.md) - Ancient necropolis carved from rock north of Paphos, a UNESCO World Heritage Site dating from the 4th century BC.
