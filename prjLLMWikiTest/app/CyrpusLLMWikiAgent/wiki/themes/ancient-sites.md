Cyprus preserves an extraordinary timeline of Mediterranean civilization, from prehistoric Neolithic villages to Greek city-states, Roman urban centers, and medieval Crusader fortifications. The island's ancient sites showcase exceptional archaeological treasures including world-renowned mosaics, rock-cut tombs, and sanctuaries dedicated to ancient gods, with several locations recognized as UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Dramatic coastal settings enhance many of these ruins, where temples and theaters overlook the sea from clifftop promontories. Whether exploring the remains of ancient capitals, walking through sacred precincts where Apollo was worshipped for over a millennium, or visiting medieval castles that once controlled the island's strategic resources, visitors encounter layer upon layer of history carved into Cyprus's landscape.

# Sites
- [Paphos Archaeological Park](/sites/paphos-archaeological-park.md) - Major archaeological site in Cyprus preserving the ancient capital of Nea Paphos with exceptional Roman mosaics and urban remains.
- [Tombs of the Kings (Paphos)](/sites/tombs-of-the-kings.md) - Ancient necropolis carved from rock north of Paphos, a UNESCO World Heritage Site dating from the 4th century BC.
- [Kourion](/sites/kourion.md) - Ancient Greek city-state on Cyprus's southwestern coast, with extensive Roman ruins atop a dramatic coastal promontory.
- [Sanctuary of Apollo Hylates](/sites/apollo-hylates.md) - Ancient sanctuary where Apollo Hylates, god of the woodlands, was worshipped from the eighth century BC to the fourth century AD.
- [Kolossi Castle](/sites/kolossi-castle.md) - A 15th-century Crusader castle near Limassol, once a strategic stronghold and center of sugar production and Commandaria wine.
- [Limassol Castle](/sites/limassol-castle.md) - A medieval castle in Limassol's old harbour, rebuilt by the Ottomans in 1590 over earlier Christian and Byzantine structures.
- [Choirokoitia](/sites/choirokoitia.md) - A UNESCO World Heritage Neolithic settlement from 7000 BC, one of the eastern Mediterranean's best-preserved prehistoric sites.
